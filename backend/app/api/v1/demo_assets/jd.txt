SENIOR CLOUD ENGINEER — Enthernet
Location: Remote (UTC ± 3)
Reports to: VP Platform

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a Senior Cloud Engineer to own the reliability, scaling,
and developer experience of the Enthernet platform. You will spend most of
your time inside a modern AWS + Kubernetes stack, deciding when to codify
things with Terraform, when to lean on managed services, and when to write
a small tool that removes a whole class of paper cuts for our engineers.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Operate our production EKS clusters (multi-account, multi-region).
- Own CI/CD on GitHub Actions and shape the deploy story from PR → prod.
- Design PostgreSQL and pgvector data patterns for AI workloads.
- Drive observability with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, and Grafana.
- Own the incident review process and improve runbooks after every page.
- Partner with product engineers on scale, security, and cost.

WHAT WE LOOK FOR
- Deep production experience with AWS and Kubernetes (5+ years).
- Comfortable writing Terraform for real infrastructure, not toy examples.
- Fluent in at least one of Python or Go; can read TypeScript.
- Track record of reducing latency, cost, or deploy time with specifics.
- Excellent judgement about trade-offs between simplicity and flexibility.
- Great communicator — writes design docs, explains decisions in plain words.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience running Kafka, Redis, or event-driven microservices.
- Familiarity with service meshes (Istio, Envoy).
- Prior on-call experience for a customer-facing service.
- Interest in AI/ML infrastructure, especially retrieval and RAG systems.

INTERVIEW LOOP
1. Screen — 45 min chat about your last two years.
2. System design — 60 min deep dive: design a service you have already run.
3. Reliability & operations — 60 min incident review and on-call scenarios.
4. Bar-raiser — 45 min culture + leadership signals.

COMPENSATION
Competitive base + equity + generous learning budget. We hire globally and
adjust to local benchmarks.
